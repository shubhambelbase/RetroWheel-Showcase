# Privacy Policy for RetroWheel

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 23, 2026

RetroWheel ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a privacy-respecting offline music player for Android. This Privacy Policy outlines our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use RetroWheel.

---

### 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
RetroWheel does not operate remote user accounts, databases, or analytics servers:
- **No Personal Data**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
- **No Music File Uploads**: Your local audio files, playlists, tags, and album art remain exclusively on your device. They are never uploaded, monitored, or transmitted to any server.

---

### 2. Device Permissions & How They Are Used
RetroWheel requests the following runtime permissions strictly to provide local functionality:
- **Audio & Media (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)**: Used solely to index and play local music files stored on your device or SD card.
- **Foreground Service & Media Playback (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`)**: Required by Android to keep playback alive in the background and display lock screen / notification media controls.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)**: Required on Android 13+ to show playback controls and current track metadata in your system notification drawer.
- **Vibration (`VIBRATE`)**: Used to drive the tactile haptic detent clicks of the rotary click wheel.
- **Network Access (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)**: Used exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve advertisements and check network connectivity.

---

### 3. Advertising & Third-Party Services
RetroWheel uses **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve advertisements:
- **Google Mobile Ads**: Google AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, coarse location, device identifiers, and ad interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
- For more details on how Google uses information from apps that use its services, visit:  
  [https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- **Zero Audio Interruption Guarantee**: In-app advertisements are configured to never interrupt active music playback.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
RetroWheel does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

---

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository or developer contact email.
